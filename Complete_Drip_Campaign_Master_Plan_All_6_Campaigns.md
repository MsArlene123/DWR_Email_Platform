## SENDING INFRASTRUCTURE NOTES

**Warm-up protocol:** All sender emails are new. For the first two weeks, throttle to one email every 7 to 10 minutes. This protects sender reputation and prevents deliverability issues with Gmail, Outlook, and corporate filters.

**Gmail daily limits:** 200 sends per day per account. Campaigns using Gmail senders must plan accordingly.

**Brevo personalization:** Use `{{contact.FIRSTNAME}}` for greetings and `{{contact.ORGANIZATION_NAME}}` for organizational personalization.

**Unsubscribe compliance:** Every email must include a visible unsubscribe link. CAN-SPAM requires physical mailing address.

**Bounce monitoring:** Watch bounce rates after every 200 sends. If any sender email exceeds 3% bounce rate, pause and investigate.

**Audience overlap warning:** Campaigns 1, 4, and 6 all target the Press Media list (818 contacts). Stagger start dates so no contact receives emails from more than one campaign on the same day. Recommended stagger: Campaign 1 starts first, Campaign 6 starts after Campaign 1 completes one cycle, Campaign 4 starts after Campaign 6 completes one cycle.

## BLOTATO SCORING CRITERIA (Applied to Email)

Every email targets 9+/10 on the Blotato grading scale.

| Criterion | Weight | Email Translation |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| Hook strength | 50% | Subject line. First 3 words determine whether the email gets opened. |
| Curiosity | 10% | Preview text + first sentence. Creates an information gap. |
| Emotional charge | 10% | Body copy. Provokes a reaction: anger, urgency, pride, recognition. |
| Share-worthiness | 10% | Would the reader forward this to a colleague? |
| Voice match | 5% | Problem-first, not credential-first. Direct, no jargon. |
| Polarity | 5% | Takes a clear position. Not neutral. |
| Platform fit | 10% | Scannable, short paragraphs, single CTA, mobile-friendly. |
