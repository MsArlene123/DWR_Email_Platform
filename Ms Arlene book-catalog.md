### 1. Finding Peace

**Category:** Christian Fiction / Spiritual  
**Published:** 2023 | **Pages:** 224

**Description:**  
When despair threatens to consume everything, sometimes the most extraordinary journeys begin. Andrea Evans stands at the precipice of her darkest hour, convinced that no one could possibly understand her overwhelming pain. With nowhere left to turn and nothing left to lose, she prepares to surrender to the despair that has become her constant companion — but in that critical moment, God hears her cry.

Through the remarkable stories of Ishita, Ho Sung, Ling, Simba, and Oliver — each carrying their own profound struggles — Andrea's perspective shifts dramatically. Their courage in the face of unimaginable hardship makes her realize a fundamental truth: she is not alone in her pain, and her story is far from over.

Andrea's journey takes an even more extraordinary turn when she becomes an unexpected witness to the Trial of Mankind itself. As Satan demands humanity's judgment before the cosmic court, Andrea finds herself thrust into the ultimate battle between good and evil, watching as the eternal fate of every human soul hangs in the balance.

*Finding Peace* is a powerful work of Christian fiction that explores themes of divine love, human resilience, and the realization that even in our most desperate moments, we are never truly alone.

**Key Features:**
- Powerful Christian fiction exploring divine love and human resilience
- Themes of spiritual warfare and divine intervention
- Journey from suicidal despair to cosmic witness
- Inspiring stories of people from the Bible who faced trials
- Exploration of God's calling and purpose for every life

**Target Audience:**
