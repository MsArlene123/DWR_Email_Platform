# WomenShield — Features, Benefits & Platform Overview

**Platform:** womenshield.com
**Mission:** Empowering women through knowledge, connection, and access to verified resources

---

## Why WomenShield Exists

Women navigating crisis, transition, financial hardship, health decisions, or career change face one overwhelming challenge: **the information they need is scattered, unverified, and hard to find under pressure.** WomenShield solves this by bringing together 6,282+ verified resources across 11 specialized directories — all in one trusted, purpose-built platform.

Whether a woman is fleeing domestic violence, starting a business, managing her health, seeking legal help, or building financial independence, WomenShield puts the right resource in front of her at exactly the right moment.

## Core Platform Features

### 1. Emergency Support — Always Visible
- Persistent crisis banner across every page with the National Domestic Violence Hotline (1-800-799-7233) and Crisis Text Line
- Emergency quick-exit button for user safety
- Dedicated Emergency Resources page with shelter, hotline, and safety guide listings
- "Leave quickly or press Esc twice" safety feature built into every page

### 2. Resource Directory — 6,282+ Verified Listings
The heart of WomenShield. Every directory is searchable, filterable by state, and regularly verified.

### 3. Employment Opportunities — Three Live Sources
- **Private sector jobs via Adzuna:** 57,000+ real-time openings searchable by keyword, location, category, and salary
- **Federal government jobs via USAJobs.gov:** 3,400+ live federal listings updated in real time
- **247+ curated roles:** Hand-picked positions in social services, healthcare, nonprofit, and women-focused organizations
- All three sources available in one page with dedicated search forms

### 4. Legal & Reproductive Rights Tracker
- State-by-state abortion law tracker
- IVF access and reproductive rights by state
- Legal aid and pro bono resources

### 5. News Feed — Real-Time
- Live articles from NewsAPI.org
- Topic filters: women's rights, health, business, legal, politics, and more
- Updated continuously

### 6. Partner Portal & Sponsorship System
- Three-tier partner system: Shield Circle Sponsor, Featured Partner, Priority Listing
- Badge designations for partners: First Responder, Discount Duty, Government Impact, Pro Bono Pledge
- Full profile pages for every paid partner with logo, description, call to action, and referral dashboard
- Application form with 9-step guided process

### 7. Donation Platform
- Stripe-integrated one-time and recurring monthly donations
- Secure, HTTPS-protected checkout

### 8. User Authentication & Dashboard
- Replit Auth integration
- Personalized user dashboard for saved resources and preferences

### 9. Interactive Emergency Resource Map
- Choropleth map showing shelter and healthcare density by state
- Seeded with 958 verified locations (585 shelters + 373 healthcare clinics)
- Color-coded by resource concentration — darker = more resources

### 10. Newsletter Subscription
- Email subscription with personalized interest selection
- Keeps women informed on platform updates and new resources

### 11. Geolocation Services
- OpenCage API integration for proximity-based search
- 373+ healthcare resources geocoded with latitude and longitude
- 585+ shelters geocoded for location-aware results

### 12. Admin Dashboard
- Content management for articles, resources, and partner data
- Donation and user data management
- Platform analytics

### 13. SEO & Discoverability
- Dynamic meta tags, Open Graph, Twitter Cards, and JSON-LD structured data via react-helmet-async
- Sitemap.xml and robots.txt
- Google Tag Manager analytics integration
- Canonical URLs and social sharing optimization

### 14. Progressive Web App (PWA)
- Service worker registration for offline capability
- Installable on mobile devices
- Fast load times with Vite build optimization

## The 11 Directories — What Makes Each Special

### 1. Healthcare & Reproductive Rights Directory
**373 verified women's healthcare clinics** across 41 states
- Every listing is geocoded with latitude/longitude for location-based search
- Covers reproductive health, OB/GYN, family planning, mental health, and general women's health
- Real-time capacity and service information where available
- State-by-state abortion and IVF law tracker integrated
- *Why it's special:* One of the only free, public directories of verified women's health providers searchable by proximity — not just by state or zip code.

### 2. Shelter & Housing Resources Directory
**585 verified women's shelters** across the United States
- Real-time capacity tracking via partner portal updates
- Covers domestic violence shelters, transitional housing, emergency housing, and long-term supportive housing
- Filterable by state, services offered, and bed availability
- Every listing verified and maintained by platform staff or partner organizations
- *Why it's special:* Most shelter databases are outdated government lists. WomenShield's are verified, geocoded, and updated by the shelters themselves through the partner portal.

### 3. Educational Resources Directory
**803 educational and business resources** across all 50 states and national programs
- Covers GED programs, community college resources, job training, skill-building, and professional development
- Includes McKinney-Vento resources for women experiencing homelessness
- Dedicated guides: McKinney-Vento Guide, Scholarships Guide, Emergency Childcare Guide, and Childcare Assistance Guide
- *Why it's special:* Bridges education access and economic independence in a single searchable directory.

### 4. Women & Their Money (Funding & Grants) Directory
**75 funding opportunities and grants** for women entrepreneurs and individuals
- Covers small business grants, individual financial assistance, emergency funds, and investment programs
- Filterable by eligibility, amount, and application status
- Linked to detailed guides on application process
- *Why it's special:* Curated specifically for women — not a generic grants database. Every listing has been evaluated for women's eligibility and relevance.

### 5. Women Businesses & Organizations Directory
**2,311 women's business organizations** across 51 states and regions (including D.C. and territories)
- Covers chambers of commerce, business associations, networking groups, incubators, and accelerators
- National and local organizations included
- Searchable by state and type of organization
- *Why it's special:* The largest curated directory of women's business organizations on any free platform — spanning every state and territory in the United States.

### 6. Employment Opportunities Directory
**57,000+ live private sector jobs** (Adzuna) + **3,400+ federal jobs** (USAJobs) + **247+ curated roles**
- Three live data sources updated in real time
- Keyword and location search for all three sources
- Category filtering for private sector: healthcare, social work, education, legal, admin, HR, finance, and more
- Federal job search includes agency, location, and pay grade filters
- Curated roles hand-picked for relevance to women's economic empowerment
- *Why it's special:* No other women's platform aggregates live private sector, live federal government, and curated listings in one place with unified search.

### 7. Fashion & Lifestyles Directory
**1,888 women-owned fashion, beauty, and lifestyle businesses**
- Covers clothing, accessories, beauty services, wellness, and lifestyle brands
- Searchable by category and state
- Supports women-owned business discovery and community building
- *Why it's special:* Economic empowerment includes supporting women entrepreneurs in consumer categories. This directory drives business to women-owned companies.

### 8. Legal Resources Directory
- Pro bono legal aid organizations
- State-specific legal rights information
- Domestic violence legal advocates
- Immigration legal resources for women
- Consumer protection and housing law resources
- *Why it's special:* Legal information and access is one of the highest barriers for women in crisis. WomenShield makes it searchable and free.

### 9. Childcare & Education Directory
- Childcare assistance programs by state
- Head Start and Early Head Start locator
- Emergency childcare resources
- After-school and summer program resources
- Resources for single mothers and working families
- *Why it's special:* Childcare access is directly tied to women's workforce participation. This directory treats childcare as an economic empowerment issue, not just a family services issue.

### 10. Healthcare Directory (Detailed — within Healthcare & Reproductive Rights)
- Planned Parenthood locations
- Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs)
- Women's health clinics and OB/GYN specialists
- Mental health providers specializing in women's trauma and postpartum care
- *Why it's special:* Each provider is individually verified — not pulled from an unmoderated aggregator — and geocoded for distance-based discovery.

### 11. Organizations & Advocacy Directory
- National women's rights organizations
- Reproductive rights advocacy groups
- Economic justice and pay equity organizations
- Political representation and leadership programs
- *Why it's special:* Connects women to movements and organizations that are working to change the systemic conditions that created the need for WomenShield in the first place.

## Technology That Powers WomenShield

- **Frontend:** React 18 with TypeScript — fast, accessible, mobile-first
- **Database:** PostgreSQL with Neon serverless — scales automatically with demand
- **Live data integrations:** Adzuna (jobs), USAJobs.gov (federal jobs), NewsAPI.org (news)
- **Geolocation:** OpenCage API — proximity search for shelters and clinics
- **Payments:** Stripe — secure donation processing
- **Authentication:** Replit Auth — secure user sessions
- **Hosting:** Replit with automatic HTTPS — always on, always secure
- **Performance:** Vite build with service worker — fast loads, offline capability
- **Accessibility:** ARIA-compliant components and semantic HTML throughout
